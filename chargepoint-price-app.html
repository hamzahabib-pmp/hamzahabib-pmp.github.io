Price App for ChargePoint Technology

As a Sales Proposal Engineer, I put together technical quotes for our customers. I built this app so the sales team can get a price for the equipment themselves, on their phone, without waiting for a full quote.

Company: ChargePoint Technology, UKRole: Designer and BuilderUsers: Sales team

The Challenge

Our equipment comes in many combinations. Each one has a component type, version, body material, operation, size and seat material, and each choice changes the price. A salesperson in a meeting often just needs a quick, reliable figure. Before this app, that meant coming back to the proposals team and waiting.

How the App Works

I kept the flow short, with one clear step on each screen.

  • Start a discovery quote. The salesperson picks their name, enters the CPT reference number and adds any meeting, company or contact notes.
  • Choose the options. Simple drop-downs for component, version, body, operation, size and seat. The salesperson also picks the currency.
  • Fetch the price. The app shows the base price, accessories, the seat add-on and the grand total, so it is clear where the number comes from.
  • Build a cart. Set the quantity and add the item to the cart. Repeat for other items, such as the passive side or accessories.
  • Review and save. The cart lists every item with its options and price, quantities can be changed or items removed, and the whole quote is saved in one tap.

Prices are blurred because they are commercially sensitive.

The Result

The sales team can now price equipment in front of the customer, with the same price logic every time. The proposals team spends less time on quick price checks and more time on the detailed quotes that need it.

Why It Matters

The same question kept coming to my desk: "What does this one cost?" I turned the answer into a tool that anyone in sales can use. Spotting that kind of repeated work and fixing it is what I enjoy most.
