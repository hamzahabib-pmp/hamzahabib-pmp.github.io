Research Dashboards in Power BI

University leadership kept asking the same questions. How many grants did we submit? How much funding did we win? Which departments are most active? I built a set of Power BI dashboards so they could answer these questions themselves.

Organisation: LUMS, PakistanRole: Head of Research OperationsTools: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Scopus

The Challenge

Research data sat in several places: the grants register, finance records and publication lists. Every board meeting meant days of pulling numbers together by hand, and by the time a report was ready, the figures were already out of date.

What I Built

Two main dashboards, refreshed from the same data the office used every day.

  • Key Research Indicators. Grants submitted and approved, funds requested and awarded, overheads, award rate and per-faculty figures, year by year from 2017 to 2023.
  • Publications. Ten years of publications from Scopus, broken down by school, department and type, including the share of papers published in the top 10% of journals.

Every page can be filtered by school, department, year, sponsor and funding source, so a dean can look at their own area in a couple of clicks.

The Result

What I Learned

A dashboard is only as good as the data behind it. Most of the real work was making the grants register clean and consistent first, which is why the SharePoint register and GAMES came before the charts.
