Research dashboards in Power BI

Leadership at LUMS kept asking the same questions: how many grants did we submit, how much did we win, which departments are active? I built a set of Power BI dashboards so they could answer those themselves.

Where: LUMS, PakistanRole: Head of Research OperationsTools: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Scopus data

The problem

Research data was spread across our SharePoint register, finance records and publication lists. Every board meeting meant someone spent days pulling numbers together by hand, and by the time the report was ready, the numbers were already old.

What I built

Two main dashboards, refreshed from the same data we used every day.

  • Key Research Indicators. Grants submitted and approved, funds requested and won, overheads, award rate, and per-faculty figures, year by year from 2017 to 2023.
  • Publications. Ten years of publications pulled from Scopus, split by school, department and type, including how many papers landed in the top 10% of journals.

Every page can be filtered by school, department, year, sponsor and funding source, so a dean can look at their own area in a couple of clicks.
