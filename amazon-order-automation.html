Amazon order automation with Power Automate

I run a small personalised gifts business. Every order meant copying details from an email into my stock sheet. So I automated it.

Where: My own business, Creative Paradigms LtdTools: Power Automate, Gmail, Google Sheets

The problem

Each Amazon order arrives as an email. I was copying the order number, product, personalisation and quantity into a Google Sheet by hand, and then updating stock. On a busy day it took a lot of time, and one typo meant a wrong product or a stock count that didn't match the shelf.

What I built

  • A Power Automate flow watches for new order emails from Amazon.
  • It pulls out the order details it needs from the email.
  • It adds a new row to my Google Sheet, which is the same sheet I use for inventory.
  • Formulas in the sheet then update stock levels, so I can see what needs reordering.

What changed

Add the time saved, e.g. "about X minutes a day" or "X hours a month", and how many orders it has handled. Fewer mistakes, and stock levels I can trust.

Why it matters for an employer

This is the same pattern most businesses need: data arrives in one place, it should end up in another, and a person is stuck in the middle copying it. I like finding those gaps and closing them.
