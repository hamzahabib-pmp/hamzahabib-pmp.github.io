FIRMS: dairy farm management system

A web application for a medium-sized dairy farm in Pakistan, covering the animals, the milk, the money and the staff. I wrote the terms of reference and delivered the system.

When: 2017Role: Scoping, project management and developmentTech: PHP, MySQL, HTML5, CSS3Timeline: 3.5 months

The problem

The farm was running on registers and notebooks. The owners had no quick way to see milk production per animal, what feed and medicine were costing, or who owed them money.

How I scoped it

Before writing any code I wrote a Terms of Reference with the client. It set out what was in scope, what was not (SMS alerts and a mobile app were left for phase two), the risks, the acceptance criteria and the payment milestones tied to sign-offs. That document kept the project on track when new ideas came up later.

What the system does

  • Cattle: each animal's record, weight, feeding, vaccinations and treatment history
  • Milk production: daily yield per animal and per time slot
  • Billing: daily milk deliveries, rates and riders for each customer
  • Inventory: feed, fuel, packaging and medicine, with expiry dates
  • Staff: payroll, advances and leave
  • Financials: chart of accounts, payments, fixed assets and profit and loss
  • Reports: sales, consumption, cost per animal and expense trends

Delivery

Three user levels (back office, staff and public), user acceptance testing against the agreed scope, a training session for the farm team, and three months of support after go-live.
