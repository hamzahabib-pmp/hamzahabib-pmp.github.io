FIRMS: Dairy Farm Management System

The Farm Information Retrieval and Management Solution (FIRMS) is a web application for a medium-sized dairy farm in Pakistan. It covers the animals, the milk, the money and the staff. I wrote the Terms of Reference and delivered the system.

Year: 2017Role: Scoping, Project Management and DevelopmentTechnology: PHP, MySQL, HTML5, CSS3Timeline: 3.5 months

The Challenge

The farm was running on paper registers and notebooks. The owners had no quick way to see milk production per animal, what feed and medicine were costing, or which customers owed them money.

How I Scoped It

Before writing any code, I agreed a Terms of Reference with the client. It set out what was in scope and what was not (SMS alerts and a mobile app were kept for phase two), the risks, the acceptance criteria and payment milestones tied to sign-offs. That document kept the project on track whenever new ideas came up.

What the System Does

  • Cattle: each animal's record, weight, feeding, vaccinations and treatment history
  • Milk Production: daily yield per animal and per time slot
  • Billing: daily milk deliveries, rates and riders for each customer
  • Inventory: feed, fuel, packaging and medicine, including expiry dates
  • Staff: payroll, advances and leave
  • Financials: chart of accounts, payments, fixed assets and profit and loss
  • Reports: sales, consumption, cost per animal and expense trends

Delivery

Separate interfaces for the back office and staff, user acceptance testing against the agreed scope, a training session for the farm team, and three months of support after go-live.
