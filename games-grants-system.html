GAMES: Grants Application, Management and Execution System

GAMES handles a research grant from the first application through to approval and tracking. It grew out of a SharePoint register I set up and ran for years.

Where: LUMS, PakistanRole: Project lead and product ownerCovers: Applications, approvals, grant records

Where it started

Before GAMES, I kept all research grant data in a SharePoint list. Each grant had a type, number, title, lead researcher, donor reference and finance code, with documents attached to the record. It worked, and it gave us one register instead of a dozen spreadsheets. But applications still came in by email and approvals happened on paper.

What we built

I wrote the requirements, worked with the development team, and acted as the product owner through build and roll-out. Researchers can now apply online in six steps:

  • Key information and donor details
  • Project details and co-investigators
  • Sub-contracting
  • Supporting documents
  • Questionnaire
  • Review and submit

Each application then goes through an approval list, so heads of department and the research office can see where it is stuck.

Moving the data

Years of grant history lived in SharePoint. I planned and ran the migration into GAMES, mapping old fields to new ones and cleaning records on the way, so nothing was lost and the Power BI reporting kept working.
