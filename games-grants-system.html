Grants Application, Management & Execution System (GAMES)

GAMES is a web-based solution for research grants administration. It is a custom-built system that helps LUMS manage and administer the research work of its faculty, from the first application through to approval and post-award tracking.

Organisation: LUMS, PakistanRole: Project Lead and Product OwnerCovers: Funding calls, applications, approvals, grant records

Where It Started

Before GAMES, I kept all research grant data in a SharePoint register. Each grant had a unique reference number, type, title, lead researcher, donor reference and finance code, with documents attached to the record. It replaced scattered files with one trusted register. However, applications still arrived by email and approvals still happened on paper.

What We Built

I wrote the requirements, worked closely with the development team, and acted as product owner through build and roll-out. GAMES brings the whole grant process into one system:

  • Funding calls and announcements. The research office publishes internal and external funding calls, with deadlines, on the GAMES home page.
  • Online applications. Researchers apply in six guided steps: key information, project details, sub-contracting, supporting documents, questionnaire, and review and submit.
  • Approvals. Each application moves through an approval list, so heads of department and the research office can see where it stands.
  • Configuration without code. Administrators manage grant types, donors, research categories, post-award attributes, forms and workflows themselves.

Moving the Data

Years of grant history lived in SharePoint. I planned and ran the migration into GAMES, mapping old fields to new ones and cleaning records along the way, so no history was lost and the Power BI reporting kept working.
