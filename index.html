About me

I am a PMP-certified project manager based in Liverpool. Most of my career was at LUMS in Pakistan, where I was Head of Research Operations for 14 years. The job was part project management, part systems, part data. I set up how research grants were tracked, built the reporting, and kept a lot of people across schools and finance on the same page.

Before that I worked at Royal Bank of Scotland in Nottingham and did freelance PHP and MySQL work. I have a BSc in Computer Science and an MSc in Management and Marketing from Nottingham Trent University.

Now I am a Sales Proposal Engineer at ChargePoint, and I run a small personalised gifts business on Amazon and Etsy. I use that business as my own lab for automation.

I am looking for roles in business systems, BI and reporting, or solutions and presales, where I can bring the project side and the data side together.
