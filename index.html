About Me

I am a PMP-certified project manager based in Liverpool, with over 16 years of experience across project management, business systems and reporting.

Most of my career was at LUMS, where I was Head of Research Operations for 14 years. The role sat between people, process and data. I set up how research grants were tracked, built the reporting, and kept faculty, schools and finance working from the same information.

Before that I worked at the Royal Bank of Scotland in Nottingham and did freelance PHP and MySQL development. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science from the University of the Punjab and an MSc in Management and Marketing from Nottingham Trent University.

Today I am a Sales Proposal Engineer at ChargePoint. I also run a small personalised gifts business on Amazon and Etsy, which I use as my own test bed for automation.

I am looking for roles in business systems, BI and reporting, or solutions and presales, where I can bring the project side and the data side together.
